iOS App · 2026

GoldTracker

A SwiftUI iOS app that pulls live spot prices for gold and silver, rendering them in minimal price cards with tabbed metals, sparkline trends, historical timeframes and real-time refresh. Built to practice MVVM architecture and async networking on Apple platforms.

  • Live spot prices via async REST API
  • Tabbed view across Gold, Silver and Platinum
  • 1D / 1W / 1M / 3M / 1Y historical trend charts
  • Clean MVVM separation: Models, ViewModels, Views
  • Native SwiftUI with light animations and pull-to-refresh
